Frequently Asked Questions

How do I file a property dispute case in Tirupati?

A property dispute is usually filed as a civil suit, for declaration of title, injunction, or recovery of possession, before the appropriate civil court. The right approach depends on the facts and documents. We assess your matter and advise on the strongest way to proceed.

What documents do I need for a property case?

Typically the sale deed and link documents, encumbrance certificate, pattadar passbook, adangal/pahani, and survey records. We tell you exactly what is needed after reviewing your situation, and help obtain missing records.

Someone is trying to grab my land, what can I do?

Act quickly. An injunction can protect your possession and prevent dispossession, and you can pursue a suit for declaration and recovery. Where land grabbing is involved, proceedings under the AP Land Grabbing (Prohibition) Act may apply.

How long does a property case take in AP?

It varies with the nature of the dispute and whether interim relief is involved. Some matters are effectively decided at the injunction stage; others go through full trial and appeal. We give you a realistic assessment at the outset.

Can you verify a property before I buy it?

Yes. This area covers title verification for flats, plots, and land before purchase, checking title, encumbrances, approvals, and land category so you buy safely.
