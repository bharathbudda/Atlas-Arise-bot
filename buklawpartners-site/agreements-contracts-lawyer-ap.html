Why Professional Drafting Matters

Most contract disputes are born at the drafting stage, in a vague clause, a missing term, or a one-sided condition that no one read carefully. A properly drafted agreement sets out the rights and obligations of each party clearly, anticipates what could go wrong, and is enforceable if it ever reaches a court. Whether you are selling property, entering a partnership, hiring a vendor, or protecting confidential information, the document is only as strong as the drafting behind it.

We draft under the framework of the Indian Contract Act, 1872, the Transfer of Property Act, the Registration Act, and the specific requirements of Andhra Pradesh, so your agreement is both practical and legally sound.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a lawyer to draft an agreement?

For anything important, property, business, or money, yes. A lawyer ensures the agreement is clear, complete, legally enforceable, and addresses the parties' interestss. A template downloaded online often misses the specific terms and legal requirements that matter in a dispute.

What is the difference between an agreement and a contract?

In practice the terms overlap. A contract is an agreement that is legally enforceable, it requires offer, acceptance, consideration, and lawful terms under the Indian Contract Act. Proper drafting is what turns an informal understanding into an enforceable contract.

Should an agreement of sale be registered?

An agreement of sale is often not compulsorily registrable, but the final sale deed must be registered under the Registration Act. Whether to register the agreement depends on the transaction, we advise on the correct approach for your situation.

Can you review a contract someone else has drafted?

Yes. We review contracts prepared by the other party, flag one-sided or risky clauses, and suggest changes so the final document is fair and protects you before you sign.

Yes. Drafting and review can be done for clients anywhere in Andhra Pradesh, with documents shared electronically. In-person consultation is available in Tirupati and Chittoor District.
