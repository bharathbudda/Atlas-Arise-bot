Buying a Flat in Tirupati: What to Check First

"Is this flat safe to buy?" is a question every home buyer should have answered before paying. The answer lies in four sets of documents: the land title, the RERA registration, the building approvals, and the builder agreement. This page explains what each should show.

Should You Get the Title Checked Before Buying a Flat?

Yes, always. A flat is the biggest purchase most families ever make, and the builder's brochure tells you nothing about whether the property is legally safe. A title assessment is a written report from an advocate on the legal position of the flat: whether the land has clear title, whether the building is approved, whether the project is RERA-registered, and whether the flat can be registered in your name without future disputes.

The cost of a proper title check is a tiny fraction of the price of the flat. The cost of skipping it can be your entire investment.

Common Question

"The builder says everything is clear, why do I need a lawyer?" Because the builder is the seller. An independent title check serves your interest, covers the documents the builder may not volunteer, and surfaces problems before the money leaves your hands.

What Is RERA and Why Does It Matter?

RERA, the Real Estate (Regulation and Development) Act, 2016, requires most residential projects to be registered with the state real estate authority before they are marketed or sold. RERA registration protects buyers by requiring the builder to disclose the project plan, timelines, approvals, and land title, and by holding the promoter accountable for delays and deviations.

Before buying a flat, we check whether the project is RERA-registered, what the registration discloses, and whether the builder is complying with RERA obligations. Buying an unregistered or non-compliant project is a serious risk, and one that is easy to avoid with a proper check.

What a Title Check on a Flat Covers

Title of the Land

We trace ownership of the land the building stands on through the sale deeds, revenue records, and encumbrance certificate (EC) to confirm clear, marketable title under the Transfer of Property Act.

RERA Registration

The project's RERA registration and what it discloses about the promoter, approvals, and timelines.

Building Plan & Approvals

We check the sanctioned building plan and municipal/panchayat permissions so you are not buying an unauthorised or deviated construction.

Encumbrance & Loans

Whether the flat and the land are subject to mortgages, bank loans, or charges that could be enforced after purchase.

Builder-Buyer Agreement

We review the agreement of sale and construction agreement, flagging one-sided clauses, hidden charges, and unfair delivery terms before you sign.

Registration Guidance

We guide you through registration of the sale deed under the Registration Act so ownership transfers to you correctly and completely.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I check whether a flat is safe to buy?

Have an advocate examine the land title, the encumbrance certificate, the sanctioned building plan, the RERA registration, and the builder agreement before you pay. This written assessment indicates whether the flat is safe to buy and what conditions to insist on.

What documents should I check before buying a flat in Tirupati?

The mother deed and link documents, current sale deed, encumbrance certificate, sanctioned building plan and layout approval, RERA registration details, and the draft agreement of sale. A first review usually shows which of these are missing.

How do I check if a project is RERA registered?

RERA registration details are maintained by the state real estate regulatory authority and should be disclosed by the builder. Verifying the registration and what it discloses about the promoter, approvals, and project timelines forms part of the title check.

How much does a title check for a flat cost?

The fee depends on the complexity of the title and the number of documents involved. It is usually small in comparison with the price of the flat.

Can you verify a flat if I am an NRI buying from abroad?

Yes. We assist NRI buyers by reviewing documents shared electronically and providing a written assessment, so the title can be examined without the buyer being physically present.

Thinking of buying a flat?

Reviewing the documents before payment is prudent. Examining the documents now can prevent years of trouble later.
