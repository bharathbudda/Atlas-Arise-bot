When a Purchase Goes Wrong

You buy a new car and it turns out to have a persistent defect the dealer will not fix. You pay for a high-value appliance or gadget that fails repeatedly. You book a flat and the builder delays possession for years. You pay for a service that is never delivered as promised. These are not just frustrations, they are legally actionable under the Consumer Protection Act, 2019.

The law recognises the consumer as the weaker party in these transactions and provides a faster, more accessible forum than ordinary civil courts: the Consumer Disputes Redressal Commission. BUK Law Partners helps consumers use it effectively.

The Right Forum for Your Claim

Consumer disputes are decided by the District, State, or National Consumer Disputes Redressal Commission depending on the value of the claim. For many product and service disputes, the Consumer Commission is faster and more cost-effective than an ordinary civil suit. For certain high-value or complex matters, a civil court action may be the stronger route. We advise you on the correct forum and the strongest way to frame your claim, because choosing the right path at the start makes a real difference to the outcome.

What You Can Recover

Depending on the matter, remedies include refund of the price, replacement of the product, free repair, removal of the defect, and compensation for the loss and hardship caused, including, in appropriate cases, for mental agony and litigation costs.

Frequently Asked Questions

I bought a defective car, what can I do?

If a vehicle has a genuine manufacturing defect that the dealer or manufacturer fails to remedy, you can file a complaint before the Consumer Commission seeking replacement, repair, refund, or compensation. The key is documenting the defect, the repair history, and the seller's failure to resolve it. We help you build and present that case.

Which consumer forum handles my complaint?

It depends on the value of the goods or services and the compensation claimed, the District, State, or National Commission has jurisdiction based on that value. We assess your matter and file before the correct forum. In some high-value or complex cases, a civil court may be the better route, and we will advise you accordingly.

How long does a consumer case take?

Consumer forums are designed to be faster than ordinary civil courts, though timelines vary with the complexity of the matter and the forum's workload. Many disputes are resolved more quickly than an equivalent civil suit would take.

What compensation can I claim as a consumer?

Depending on the facts, you may claim refund of the amount paid, replacement of the defective product, free repair or removal of the defect, and compensation for the loss, inconvenience, and mental agony caused, along with the cost of litigation.
