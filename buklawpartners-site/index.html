BUK Law Partners was founded on the conviction that complex legal problems demand structured, research-driven solutions. With a base in Tirupati and an active presence before the High Court of Andhra Pradesh at Amaravati, the firm handles a wide spectrum of litigation. The firm has been in practice since 1995.

Our advocates bring deep expertise in procedural law and statutory interpretation, built through years of trial court and High Court practice. We handle property title disputes, writ petitions under Article 226, SARFAESI and DRT proceedings, civil appeals, commercial contract disputes, and revenue court matters across Andhra Pradesh.

Whether you need an property dispute lawyer in Tirupati, a High Court advocate in Andhra Pradesh, or expert legal advice on debt recovery, startup law, or banking matters, our team has the depth and standing to represent you effectively.

We serve clients across Tirupati, Chittoor District, Chandragiri, Renigunta, Srikalahasti, Puttur and the surrounding areas, and appear before the High Court of Andhra Pradesh at Amaravati, the Debt Recovery Tribunal at Hyderabad, and courts and tribunals throughout the state.

Our Approach

Why BUK Law Partners

Six reasons clients trust us with their most important legal matters, from first advice to final order.

I

Structured Case Analysis

Every matter begins with a thorough review of documents, a fact-pattern analysis, and a clear assessment of strengths, risks, and strategy, before a single step is taken in court.

II

Mastery of AP Law

Our practice is rooted in the specific statutes, government orders, and judicial trends that govern Andhra Pradesh, from the Estates Abolition Act to SARFAESI and beyond.

III

Interim Relief When It Matters

Protecting your rights at the threshold stage is often the most critical moment in litigation. We are experienced in obtaining stays, injunctions, and ad-interim orders under time pressure.

IV

End-to-End Representation

From the first legal notice to the final order, we handle every stage of your matter, drafting, filing, arguing, and follow-through on execution.

V

Transparent Communication

We keep clients informed at each stage with clear, plain-language updates on case developments, court dates, and strategic decisions. No surprises.

VI

Multi-Forum Practice

Whether your matter belongs in a trial court, the High Court, a DRT, or a revenue tribunal, we have the experience and standing to represent you effectively across all forums in AP.
